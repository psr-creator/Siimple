## 2. Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

**Geography:** UAE (Dubai, Sharjah, Abu Dhabi, Ajman, RAK; mainland + free zones such as JAFZA, DAFZA, KIZAD, SAIF, Hamriyah).

| Segment | Best offer | Size sweet spot | Pain signals |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Trading / distribution** | BC (+ WMS if they have a warehouse) | 20–300 staff, AED 20M–500M revenue | Excel/Tally, stock mismatch, no landed-cost, multi-branch, VAT/e-invoicing worries |
| **Construction / contracting** | BC + Projects (job costing) | 50–1,000 staff | Project cost overruns, subcontractor billing, progress (IPC) billing, retention, plant/equipment tracking |
| **Manufacturing** | BC Premium (Manufacturing) | 30–500 staff | No BOM/routing control, production costing guesswork, wastage, MRP done in Excel |
| **Services** | BC (+ Projects/Service) | 20–200 staff | Timesheets, contract billing, multi-entity consolidation |
| **3PL / 4PL / freight forwarders / e-commerce fulfilment** | InsightPro WMS (+ FMS, + BC) | Any warehouse with 2+ clients or 5,000+ SKUs | Multi-client billing leakage, no client portal, picking errors, no RFID, Amazon/Noon/Shopify sync issues |

**Disqualifiers:** fewer than 5 users and no growth plans; wants "free" or Odoo Community pricing; no decision-maker accessible; needs go-live in under 4 weeks.

**Buying committee (typical UAE SME):** Owner/MD or GM (economic buyer, often the final say on price), CFO/Finance Manager (champion for ERP), Operations/Warehouse Manager (champion for WMS), IT Manager (technical gatekeeper), Group/Family office (multi-entity groups).

## 6. Voice & style for all customer-facing text
- Concise, direct, confident. No fluff, no "I hope this email finds you well".
- Business outcomes first (stock accuracy, cash flow, cost per project, compliance), features second.
- UAE-appropriate courtesy ("Dear Mr./Ms. <Surname>" for first contact with senior people; first names once rapport exists).
- Emails ≤120 words for cold outreach; one clear call to action.
- WhatsApp is normal for follow-ups in the UAE: keep those to 2–3 lines.
- When rewriting a rep's draft: keep their tone, make **minimal edits** unless asked for a rewrite.
